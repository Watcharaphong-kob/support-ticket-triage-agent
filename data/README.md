# Prototype data

sample_tickets.json preserves all three assignment conversations, including twelve messages, relative times, original Thai and separately supplied translations. The numbered source_sample links to the original assignment; ticket/customer IDs are synthetic. There are no inferred dates, product names, urgency labels, or action answers in the inputs.

customers.json contains synthetic history matching only the supplied account context. Null means unknown. Billing and outage statements are customer reports, not verified payment or incident records. First critical issue does not mean first-ever support contact.

knowledge_base.json contains synthetic illustrative policies for retrieval demonstrations; none establish actual company policy, refunds, live outages, or feature availability.
